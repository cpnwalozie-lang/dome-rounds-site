How a match goes

You and your rival start with a basic blaster and a dome. Every round makes you both stronger in different ways.

  1. 1

    Fight

    Blast energy shots, pop your dome to block, dash and jump around the arena.

  2. 2

    Pick a card

    After each knockout you get three cards and one minute. Take the one your build needs.

  3. 3

    Stack it

    Every card stacks with everything. Five rockets on one shot? Sure. Frozen black holes? Why not.

First to five rounds wins.

57 cards. Every one stacks.

Blaster cards change your shots. Body cards change you. Dome cards go off when you block. Aura cards hurt anyone who gets close.

It gets loud

Every card has its own effect, and they all show up in the fight.

Five arenas

Dockside

A sunset harbour full of containers, ramps and a long drop into the sea.

Neon City

A rooftop arena above a glowing city. Mind the edge.

The Crane

A deck swinging off a crane over the city. Too many of you on one side and it tips.

Foundry

Steel floor, lava under it. Dodge the press, the swinging spike balls and the floor saws.

Neon Crossroads

The free-for-all map. Four corner bases, one tower in the middle, and only one of you walks away.

Play a friend with a code

No accounts, no lobbies to scroll through. Just send a code.

  1. Press Play Online and create a room.
  2. Send your friend the 5-character code.
  3. They type it in and the fight starts.

Bring friends: 2v2 teams (blue vs orange, no friendly fire) or Free for all with 3 or 4 players, last one standing wins the round. Short a player? The host can fill the seat with a bot.

Or play bots on Easy, Normal or Hard, or try every card in the training sandbox.
